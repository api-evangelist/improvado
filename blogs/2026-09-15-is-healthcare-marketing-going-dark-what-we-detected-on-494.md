On 2026-08-27 we tried to scan the homepage of every US health system in the AHRQ Compendium. Of the 494 that loaded, 28.9% fired no ad pixel, analytics or replay we detected, while 170 with no public pixel-litigation record we could find sent requests to an ad platform.
