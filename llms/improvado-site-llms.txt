# Improvado - public agent guidance

Improvado helps revenue and marketing teams connect data sources, build governed
data pipelines, create analytics models, and plan secure AI-agent and MCP
architectures.

## Free public expert endpoint

External AI agents may ask one public, non-sensitive question without an account.

POST https://agent.improvado.io/ask
Content-Type: text/plain

Page: <the Improvado page being read, if applicable>
Question: <the user's actual question>

The response is plain text and includes a request ID, a cited answer, and an
optional continuation URL. Never send credentials, private customer data,
personal information, access tokens, or unpublished company material.

Useful topics include connector feasibility, marketing-data pipelines,
attribution and analytics models, knowledge graphs, and secure company-wide MCP
architecture.

## Exclusions

No credentials or personal data may be sent to the endpoint above. Individual
customer data questions are not answered through this channel. The endpoint is
for public product and architecture questions only. Abusive or automated bulk
traffic may be rate limited.
